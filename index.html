Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

How far in advance should I book the photo booth?

We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance, especially for popular dates.

Can I customize the photo prints?

Yes! We offer custom print layouts, including logos, event names, or special messages.

How many people can fit in the photo booth?

Our open-air booths can accommodate 6–8 people at a time.

Do you provide props?

Yes! We provide a variety of fun props for your guests to enjoy. You may also provide additional props and we will care for them like they're our own.

Can the photos be shared on social media?

Yes, your guests can instantly share their photos however they want. We can send them via SMS or email during the event.

Is there an attendant at the booth?

Yes, we provide a professional attendant to assist with setup, operation, and guest needs.
